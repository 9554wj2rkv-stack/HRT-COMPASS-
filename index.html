Movement & Menopause — Stronger, One Step at a Time

▾
Strength training — Build your strength

Strength training can help preserve muscle, support bone health and maintain everyday strength during perimenopause and menopause. You do not need to lift heavy weights to begin. Resistance bands, machines, bodyweight exercises and lighter weights can all be useful.

Walking — Every step counts

Walking supports cardiovascular health, stamina and general wellbeing. It is also a weight-bearing activity that supports bone health. Start with a comfortable duration and build gradually.

Recovery — Rest is part of progress

Your body needs time to recover. Sleep, rest and adjusting activity to your energy levels are important. Some days a short walk may feel right; on other days rest may be more appropriate.

Movement is not a competition. Start where you are, choose what feels manageable, and build consistency over time.

General educational information, not a personalised exercise plan. Movement does not replace HRT or guarantee symptom relief — check with your doctor before starting something new.
