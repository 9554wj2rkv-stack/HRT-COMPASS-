Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you

Everything you enter in HRT Compass — your check-ins, symptoms, HRT and doses, cycle and period history, bleeding, sleep, weight, notes and settings — is saved only on the device you are using, inside the app itself.

We never see it

Your records are never sent to us or to any server. There is no account, no login, no email sign-up and no cloud copy. HRT Compass has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your information is never sold or shared, and we cannot see or recover it.

When the app loads

HRT Compass is delivered to your device by its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, the first time you open it and whenever it updates. Like any website, the hosting provider can see basic technical details such as your device’s internet (IP) address, but never your health information. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The app’s fonts are included inside the app itself, so no other outside services are contacted. After it has loaded, HRT Compass works offline and your entries stay on your device.

Your backups and control

  • Export a full backup or a CSV copy of your data (Report → Data backup).
  • Import a backup to restore it — your current data is safely backed up first.
  • Delete everything from this device at any time (Report → Clear all data).

Please keep your own backups

Because your data lives only in the app on this device, it can be lost if you clear your browser or website data, delete the app, change device, or if your device is damaged. We cannot recover data for you and do not promise permanent storage — so please keep your own exported backups.

Who is responsible

HRT Compass is published under the name North Spark 21, based in Denmark.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you may have rights under the GDPR. Because the app does not send your health data to us, we do not hold those records; any technical data handled by the providers above is covered by their own policies.

Medical Information

HRT Compass is a personal tracking and educational tool — it is here to help you notice and record how you feel over time.

It is intended as a tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, does not prescribe or recommend medication or doses, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Any estimates — such as estimated ovulation, fertile windows or symptom patterns — are approximate, are based only on the information you enter, and are always labelled as estimates. They should not be used for contraception, or to plan or avoid pregnancy.

Please talk to your doctor or another qualified healthcare professional about your symptoms and any changes to your HRT or medication. For anything urgent, contact your doctor or local emergency services.
